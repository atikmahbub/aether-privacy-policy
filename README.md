# Privacy Policy for Scalar: Expense Tracker

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026

Scalar collects only what it needs to track your money, never sells your data, and lets you delete everything at any time.

Scalar: Expense Tracker ("Scalar", "we", "our", "us") is a personal finance app for Android, developed by Doodle Dash Labs. Scalar was previously named Aether. This policy explains what information Scalar collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Scalar app on Google Play (package `com.atik.aether`) and any related support channels.

---

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use these only to create your account and show who is signed in.

**Financial records you enter.** Everything you add yourself, including:

- Expenses and income, with amounts, categories, dates and notes
- Monthly spending limits and budgets
- Loans you give or take, including any names or notes you add about the other person
- Investments and other financial records

Scalar never connects to your bank, card or mobile wallet. It only stores what you type in.

**App settings.** Preferences such as currency, categories, appearance and the carry over setting, saved to your account so they work across devices.

**Feedback you send.** If you use "Send feedback", Scalar opens your own email app with your message ready to send. The email includes the app version, device model and Android version to help fix problems. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app, and you can edit or remove any of it first.

**Technical data.** Basic logs needed to run the service, such as errors and request times on our servers.

---

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information only to run Scalar for you:

- Sign you in and keep your account secure
- Store, sync and show your records across your devices
- Calculate totals such as spent, remaining, carry over and daily amounts
- Reply to feedback and fix bugs
- Keep the service stable and improve performance

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it, or use it to build profiles about you. Scalar shows no ads.

---

## 3. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only with services needed to run the app:

| Service | Why | What it handles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Sign-In | Login | Name, email, profile picture |
| Cloud hosting and database | Storing your account and records | All data in section 1 |
| Google Play | App distribution and updates | Handled under Google's own policy |

These providers process data on our behalf and are not allowed to use it for their own purposes. We may also disclose data if required by law.

---

## 4. Storage, security and retention

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers and linked to your account by a unique user ID. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to the database is limited to what is needed to run and maintain the service.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed as described in section 5. Feedback emails stay in our inbox until no longer needed to handle your request.

---

## 5. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in two ways:

1. **In the app:** Settings → Delete account
2. **By email:** write to [atikmahbub100@gmail.com](mailto:atikmahbub100@gmail.com) from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my Scalar account"

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and all your financial records, including expenses, income, limits, loans, investments and settings. This cannot be undone. Email requests are handled within 30 days.

---

## 6. Your rights

You can ask to see a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. Most of this you can do directly in the app by editing or deleting records. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

---

## 7. Children's privacy

Scalar is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
